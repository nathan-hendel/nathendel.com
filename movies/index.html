Imposter Syndrome

Short film · 2016

Here's a short film I made about imposter syndrome, the unwarranted feeling of inadequacy which is prevalent in academic settings. The film was made for the UCSF Quantitative Biology Consortium Retreat in 2016, and later it would play on the TVs in the waiting room at the UCSF Student Health Clinic.
